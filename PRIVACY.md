# Privacy Policy: Anatomo

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Anatomo is made by Lumio Apps. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Anatomo does not collect, send or sell any personal data.

## What stays on your phone

Your language, the body you chose (male or female) and the layers you turned on are saved in a small file inside the app. They never leave your phone. They are deleted only when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Updates keep them.

## Internet

- **3D viewer.** The body is drawn by a tiny web server that runs inside the app at `127.0.0.1`. It is only reachable from your own phone. All anatomy data is inside the app, so exploring works fully offline.
- **Check for updates.** Only when you tap it, the app asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release of `lumio-apps/anatomo` and, if you agree, downloads the new APK from GitHub. GitHub may log the request as with any website visit. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Permissions

- `INTERNET`: for the local 3D viewer and for "Check for updates".
- `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES`: to open a downloaded update so Android can install it. Android asks you before installing.

## Children

The app has no accounts and collects no data from anyone, including children.
